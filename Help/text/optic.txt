Undermining / The excavation (cup) doesn't just go down; it eats away at the sides underneath the neuroretinal rim. Imagine a cliff where the ocean has eroded the base. The top of the cliff now "overhangs" a hollow space. That hollow space is the undermining.
Bayoneting /As a vessel comes from the retina toward the center of the nerve, it reaches the edge of the cup and has to make a sharp, double turn to follow the new, hollowed-out shape of the nerve.The vessel appears to "disappear" under the overhanging rim and then reappears at the bottom of the cup, shifted to one side
Nasal displacement / The movement of the central retinal vessels toward the nasal side (toward the nose) of the optic disc.
Baring/A vessel no longer touches the neural rim because the tissue underneath has eroded. It looks like it's "floating" over a space.
Lamilar dot sign / Small greyish dots at the bottom of the optic cup.
Notching / Focal, localized loss of the neuroretinal rim. - A "bite-like" defect or a notch in the rim localized at Inferior-temporal or superior-temporal poles.
Zone Alpha / Irregular pigmentation of the RPE at the outer periphery of the optic disc.- (dark and light mottling of the RPE).
Beta zone / A region of complete atrophy of the Retinal Pigment Epithelium (RPE) and the choriocapillaris. It sits inside Zone Alpha, touching the margin of the optic disc
